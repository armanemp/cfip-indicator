# Reference Coverage

The complete reference behavior is the source of truth for parity.

Coverage includes:

- M1/M5/M15/M30/H1/H4/D1/W1 closed-bar MTF context;
- indicator stack, trend, momentum and volatility interpretation;
- market quality, regime and confluence;
- structure, BOS, MSS, CHOCH and displacement;
- FVG, Order Block, supply/demand zones and mitigation;
- equal highs/lows and liquidity sweep logic;
- precision entry zone, ideal entry and trigger;
- retest and breakout/continuation entry modes;
- structural invalidation;
- structural stop and reward target aggregation;
- HTF/liquidity target selection and RR validation;
- automatic market execution;
- automatic stop/limit pending orders;
- idempotency and managed broker identity;
- pending-order and position lifecycle;
- broker reconciliation and missing-protection recovery;
- structural live stop/target management;
- profit-exhaustion, reversal and invalidation exits;
- partial take-profit and break-even handling;
- daily-loss, margin, session, spread, event and market-suitability guards;
- early prediction;
- outcome telemetry and confidence calibration;
- chart levels, arrows, prediction objects and historical rendering;
- unified panel, popup and semantic alerts;
- close/cancel safety controls.

Parity means these behaviors exist once, under one owner, with no duplicated authority.
